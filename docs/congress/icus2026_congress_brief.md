## Theme Fit

The call frames resilient cities through climate impacts, social inequalities, spatial justice, local governance, industrial transformation, green infrastructure, and adaptation policy. A PlanX Urban Resilience paper can fit especially well under:

- Climate change, urban vulnerabilities, and social inequalities
- Resilient cities and urban policies
- Governance, participation, and local governments
- Nature, urban ecosystems, and climate justice
- Spatial justice and right to the city
- Climate crisis and disaster risk
- Resilience and sustainability policies

## Submission Rules for the Abstract

- Submission is abstract-first.
- Abstract length: **500-750 words**.
- The abstract must be submitted through CMT; the guidance also mentions a Word-format upload, so the practical workflow should prepare both CMT text and a Word-compatible file.
- The abstract should state aim, method, findings or expected findings, and originality.
- Abstract text should not include figures, images, tables, references, or author-identifying information.
- Keywords: minimum 3, maximum 5.
- Turkish submissions require English title, abstract, and keywords as well.
- English submissions require Turkish title, abstract, and keywords as well.
- The subject must align with the main theme and subthemes.

## Review and Ethics

- Review is double blind.
- Evaluation criteria include originality, scientific contribution, methodological adequacy, findings, and writing quality.
- Similarity should be below 20 percent, checked before submission with tools such as Turnitin or iThenticate.
- Required declarations later include conflict of interest, author contribution, ethics, copyright, funding/acknowledgement where relevant.
- The ethics text is strict about AI-generated content. The safe workflow is to use AI only for planning, compliance checking, translation alternatives, and critique; final submitted prose should be author-owned and manually verified.

## Presentation Rules After Acceptance

- Standard oral format: maximum 15 minutes presentation + 5 minutes Q&A.
- Slides should be PowerPoint-compatible and 16:9.
- Presentation files should be delivered to the control desk 90 minutes before the session.
- Visuals should be high resolution; the page specifies 300 DPI.

## Publication Route

- Accepted and presented abstracts are planned for the Abstract Book.
- Full-text submission is optional after the congress.
- Authors may submit expanded papers to IdealKent according to that journal's policies.
- IdealKent route uses APA 7.0 and requires the note that the work was presented at the 11th Urban Studies Congress.
