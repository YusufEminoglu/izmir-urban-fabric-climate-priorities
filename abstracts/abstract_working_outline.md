## Abstract Structure Target

Target length: 500-750 words.

1. **Problem:** climate resilience is shaped not only by hazards, but also by urban tissue, street configuration, density, openness, accessibility, and social vulnerability.
2. **Gap:** many urban resilience assessments stay at administrative-unit scale and miss the street/design scale at which urban form is produced and experienced.
3. **Aim:** test an open-source QGIS workflow for classifying street-based urban tissue samples and relating them to resilience capacity.
4. **Case:** Izmir Gulf or Izmir functional urban region, with selected samples representing contrasting urban fabrics.
5. **Method:** PlanX main urban morphology/network tools supported by PlanX Urban Resilience modules; narrated as a PlanX Urban Resilience workflow.
6. **Outputs:** tissue typology, morphology/accessibility profiles, heat or microclimate exposure proxies, social/access deficits, adaptation-priority classes.
7. **Originality:** bridges urban morphology and climate resilience through an open-source, QGIS-verifiable workflow.
8. **Expected contribution:** gives urban design and planning researchers a replicable way to compare the resilience implications of different urban fabrics.
